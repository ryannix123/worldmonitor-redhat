# World Monitor — the box on the shelf

**What it is.** A small computer running World Monitor, an open-source global
situational-awareness dashboard (conflicts, military activity, shipping,
markets, news — one screen). It is a personal project running free, open-source
software. It updates itself overnight and needs no maintenance.

**Open it.** From any computer on this network:

    http://wm-armory.local:3000        (or the IP address written below)

Bookmark that. Add `?view=global&timeRange=24h` to the address to get the
24-hour global view.

**If the screen looks stuck or blank.**
1. Wait five minutes — it may be mid-update (it updates around 5 a.m. and
   reboots early Sunday mornings).
2. Still stuck? Unplug the power, count to ten, plug it back in. It comes back
   on its own in about two minutes; panels fill in over the next 15.
3. Still stuck after that? Text the number below. We'll do a quick screen-share.

**Please don't.** Log in to it, plug drives into it, or move it to a network it
wasn't set up on. There's nothing to configure — it's an appliance.

**Costs nothing to run** beyond electricity and a few dollars a month of AI
credits that are already covered.
